Worker live

Goal-conditioned worker (train_worker.py), real time. Each rider chases a random target: dashed line = where its body should be, diamond = a point in the air its body should pass through (a jump), ring = where its pole tip should be (drawn in the rider's colours); the line turns solid / the ring fills while that part is satisfied. A rider with both only scores once both are satisfied at the same moment (body within 0.75 m in x, tip within 1 m); it earns a little for progress toward each part meanwhile. Unreached targets are dropped after 6 s and a new one is drawn.

Game mods

  

      
Applies to the real game (Sportsfriends): patches the game binary from mods.toml (a backup of the original is kept) and sets the match format in config.ini; restart the game to see it. Snake / pencil / Christmas / Chinese New Year are cosmetic; the hidden 8th character has a shorter pole and stiffer arms. Training uses the native simulator and is not affected.

Plug in your controller first and don't press anything while the menus are scripted. Your recorded play (sticks every frame + the state every 4th) goes to runs/human/ and becomes training data (analysis/playlog.py). 

    

  





  

    
Population 

    

      
Fitness = mean goal difference per match in the last tournament, against every other member and the anchors (farm2, farm3), 32 matches per pairing, 7 goals / 5 min. After each tournament the worst member takes over the best one's weights with a perturbed genome (if the best leads by more than 0.25). Each member has its own tab (training charts, exhibitions vs farm2).

Ratings 

    

      
Elo from every member pairing (Bradley-Terry on win / draw / loss, a draw counts half; farm2 latest = 1500; +200 Elo ≈ wins 3 of 4). 64 matches per pairing, sides alternating, 7 goals / 5 min, a tie at the end of time is a draw. Goal difference = goals per match from that policy's view. While the round robin is still filling, ratings move.

  

  

    
Rated candidates

    

      
Each candidate played every member (gauntlet.py rate) and is rated with the members' ratings held fixed, so candidates rated at different times stay comparable.

  

  

    
Head-to-head

    

      
Goal difference per match from the row's view (green = row wins); hover a cell for wins-draws-losses and kills. Sorted by Elo. Blank = not played yet; – = hierarchical vs hierarchical (not supported).

  

  

    
Exploitability

    

      
A copy of the target trained only against the frozen target (gauntlet.py exploit). Its goal difference against the target afterwards is how exploitable the target is: 0 = nothing found, higher = a counter-strategy exists.
