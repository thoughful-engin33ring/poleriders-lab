== 5. Pole rotation (the user: t20 'doesn't want to move his pole clockwise' in [t20, user, ball]) and the pole servo
  Frame: every number is in the rider's attack frame (0 deg = toward the goal it attacks, 90 = up). Backward = counter-clockwise there = CLOCKWISE ON SCREEN for t20 (it attacks left), counter-clockwise on screen for the user.
  How the stick drives the pole (sim/src/game/player.cpp pole servo, input.cpp; checked in the sim below):
   - target = atan2(stick y, stick x): the stick sets an ABSOLUTE pole angle. The error to the handle's angle is wrapped to
     +-180 deg, so the pole always turns the SHORT way; motor speed and max torque both scale with the error (kp 0.06, kd 0.004,
     clamp 0.4: never reached by the error alone), i.e. the push is strongest just under 180 deg ahead and flips direction past it.
   - per-axis dead zone 0.1, then the servo works only if the stick length > 0.2: a centred stick = zero torque, the pole
     hangs free ('letting go'). A policy's (aim x, aim y) maps to the stick directly (x mirrored for the right rider).
   - so a full backward rotation needs the stick swept round backward, kept ahead of the pole by < 180 deg the whole way
     (a human: a continuous circular motion of the thumb). Aiming straight 'behind' does not rotate backward: from a
     forward-down pole, any aim more than 180 deg away going backward makes the pole go FORWARD (demo).

== 7. Training ideas for the weaknesses above (suggestions only, nothing implemented)
  - Start states from situation A (the named [t20, user, ball]): a share of resets (like --pit-frac / --goal-spawn) that put one rider past the ball with the opponent between it and the ball, incl. the stacked variant (the opponent standing on its upright pole) — or restore real states harvested at A entries / interlock starts (NativeHost.snapshot/restore, the sim and the 16 real logs). Measure: P(conceded 10 s) and 'back on the good side in 3 s' from A (section 4).
  - Action parameterization for the pole: aim RELATIVE to the current pole angle (a signed rotation offset in (-180, 180) plus a strength) instead of an absolute stick angle. The servo already works on the error to the aimed angle, so 'keep rotating backward' becomes one constant output instead of a stick sweep that has to stay < 180 deg ahead of a moving pole; a forward-biased Gaussian on (aim x, aim y) (t20 drives forward ~75% of the time) rarely produces it. Could be an --action-head option, A/B-tested like gated/resmlp.
  - A real 'let go' action: bots almost never centre the stick (servo off 1-2% of play; the user 30%), because a Gaussian over (aim x, aim y) seldom lands in the 0.28 dead box. A discrete release bit (or the gated head's gate on the aim, if n1_t20_gated has one) makes letting go learnable; check whether n1_t20_gated ever releases in interlocks.
  - Concept prediction targets (auxiliary heads, no reward change): from each state predict 'conceded within 10 s', 'back on the good side within 3 s' (from A), the outcome of the current interlock (who kicks first after it), and open-goal conversion; plus the concept states themselves (sit_A, interlocked, open_goal) 1 s ahead.
  - Interlocks: t20 self-play treats them as safe for the attacker (scores 30% / concedes 2% within 5 s after) and holds some to the final whistle (4.5 min); humans break them by letting go. A league opponent that releases its pole in interlocks (a scripted override of the frozen opponent's aim while interlocked) would price that in. Note the user rejected random do-nothing opponents; this is a targeted expert move, so it needs the user's call.
  - Open-goal finishing: kill-window start states (opponent dead with 1-3 s of respawn left, ball at various x) and opponent-out-of-position states, scored by conversion within the window; the sim conversion (t20 43%, farm2 28%) is the baseline to beat.
  - Behaviour cloning on the user's demos (runs/human/demos.npz) restricted to A / interlock states as an auxiliary loss: the user's backward drive in A (54% of the time vs t20 7%) and his let-gos are in those rows.

  thresholds: interlock >= 1 s, gaps <= 0.33 s, long >= 3 s; let-go window 0 s; open goal dx 5.5 m, grace 1 s; servo dead zone 0.1, off below stick length 0.2
